## ✨ Overview

**Ambiencer** is a high-performance native cross-platform application built with **Tauri v2, Rust, WebAudio API, and Dark Glassmorphism UI**. Designed for creators, developers, students, yoga practitioners, and sound therapists:

1. 🎛️ **100% Offline HD Soundscape Mixer:** Mix 16+ high-fidelity audio loops (Window Rain, Storm & Thunder, Ocean Waves, Fireplace, Bamboo Fountain, Night Forest, Mystical Cave, ASMR Keyboard & Pencil, and procedural Pink/White noise) with **0ms Equal-Power Crossfade Looping**.
2. 🔮 **Sacred Solfeggio & Binaural Beats Synthesizer:** Real-time synthesis of pure Solfeggio frequencies (174Hz, 432Hz, 528Hz, 639Hz, 741Hz, 852Hz) and customizable Binaural Beats (Delta 2.5Hz, Theta 6.0Hz, Alpha 10.0Hz, Beta 20.0Hz, Gamma 40.0Hz) with custom waveforms and direct pure signal routing.
3. ⭐ **Custom Preset Management:** Create, name, badge, and save custom soundscape mixes with instant 1-click loading and persistent local storage.
4. 🌌 **Live Procedural Canvas Wallpapers:** 12 real-time atmospheric 60 FPS visualizers (Wet Glass Rain, Aurora Stars, Fireplace Glow, Cyber Grid, Zen Ripple, etc.).
5. 🪟 **Interactive Floating Desktop Widgets (Windows):** Drag-and-drop glass widgets (Digital Clock, World Timezones, System Monitor Lite, Post-It Notes) pinned over your desktop with 60fps smooth dragging.
6. 📱 **Native Home Screen Widget & Screensaver (Android):** Ultra-fast 2x1 glass widget for instant Play/Pause and full-screen Zen Screensaver with Sleep Timer integration.
7. 🌐 **Bilingual i18n:** Instant 0ms toggle between Spanish (ES) and English (EN).
