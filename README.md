## ✨ Overview

**Ambiencer** is a high-performance native Windows desktop application built with **Tauri v2, Rust, WebAudio API, and Dark Glassmorphism UI**. Designed for creators, developers, students, yoga practitioners, and sound therapists:

1. 🎛️ **100% Offline HD Soundscape Mixer:** Mix 16 high-fidelity audio loops (Window Rain, Storm & Thunder, Ocean Waves, Fireplace, Bamboo Fountain, Night Forest, Mystical Cave, ASMR Keyboard & Pencil, and procedural Pink/White noise) with **0ms Equal-Power Crossfade Looping**.
2. 🔮 **Sacred Solfeggio & Binaural Beats Synthesizer:** Real-time synthesis of Solfeggio frequencies (174Hz, 432Hz, 528Hz, 639Hz, 741Hz, 852Hz) and customizable Binaural Beats (Delta 2.5Hz, Theta 6.0Hz, Alpha 10.0Hz, Beta 20.0Hz, Gamma 40.0Hz) with custom waveforms and lowpass filtering.
3. 🧘 **Guided Breathwork & Yoga Suite (Pranayama):** Animated glowing glass widget for **Relaxation 4-7-8**, **Box Breathing 4x4**, and **Cardiac Coherence 5-5**.
4. ⭐ **Custom Preset Management:** Create, name, badge, and save custom soundscape mixes with instant 1-click loading and persistent local storage.
5. 🌌 **Live Procedural Canvas Wallpapers:** 4 real-time atmospheric WebGL wallpapers (Wet Glass Rain, Aurora Stars, Fireplace Glow, Cyber Grid).
6. 🪟 **Interactive Floating Desktop Widgets:** Drag-and-drop glass widgets (Digital Clock, Pomodoro Focus Timer, System Monitor Lite, Post-It Notes, and Breathwork Guide) pinned over your desktop with 60fps smooth dragging.
7. 🌐 **Bilingual i18n:** Instant 0ms toggle between Spanish (ES) and English (EN).
