## 📝 15 Mini-Prompts Listos para Usar en IA

Copia y pega estos prompts directamente en la IA para obtener capas de audio modulares perfectas:

### 🌧️ Naturaleza & Clima
1. **Lluvia en Ventana:**
   `Gentle raindrops falling steadily on a glass window pane, soft wind background, crisp audio, seamless loop`
2. **Tormenta con Truenos:**
   `Heavy thunderstorm rumble, distant low frequency thunder claps, dark rainy night ambience, high detail`
3. **Olas de Mar Calmas:**
   `Calm ocean waves gently washing ashore on a pebble beach, smooth rhythm, binaural stereo depth`
4. **Viento en Bosque de Pinos:**
   `Soft autumn wind blowing through pine forest leaves and branches, peaceful nature soundscape`
5. **Fogata Acogedora:**
   `Crackle of a cozy fireplace, warm burning wooden logs, subtle ember sparks popping, crisp warmth`

### ☕ Ambientes Urbanos & Espacios
6. **Cafetería de Fondo:**
   `Cozy coffee shop interior ambience, muffled distant chatter, gentle ceramic cups clinking, no distinct words`
7. **Biblioteca Silenciosa:**
   `Quiet library interior, subtle paper pages turning, soft ambient room tone, relaxing focus atmosphere`
8. **Viaje en Tren Nocturno:**
   `Passenger train interior, gentle rhythmic rail hum moving steadily on tracks, cozy travel vibration`
9. **Lluvia dentro de Auto:**
   `Night rain falling on car windshield, soft windshield wipers moving rhythmically, wet pavement background`

### ⌨️ ASMR & Enfoque Tactil
10. **Teclado Mecánico Thocky:**
    `ASMR mechanical keyboard typing, thocky custom lubricated switches, steady productivity pace, close mic`
11. **Escritura con Lápiz:**
    `Pencil writing smoothly on thick paper notebook, crisp tactile paper friction ASMR, relaxing study`
12. **Ronroneo de Gato:**
    `Cat purring sleeping peacefully, rhythmic soothing low frequency vibration, cozy warm bedroom`

### 🌌 Fantasía & Espacio Zen
13. **Espacio Profundo (Pad Ambient):**
    `Deep space cosmic ambient pad drone, smooth warm sub bass, zero noise, meditative zen focus`
14. **Buceo Submarino:**
    `Underwater diving ambience, deep muffled ocean bubbles, gentle water resonance, dark ocean depth`
15. **Fuente de Bambú Zen:**
    `Japanese bamboo water fountain shishi-odoshi, gentle flowing water stream, water drop taps`
